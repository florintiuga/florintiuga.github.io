Event Assistant

A personal Android app that reads new emails in your own Gmail (and optionally Outlook) account, finds events, bills, deliveries, tickets and deadlines, and lets you add them to your phone's calendar and to Asana.

Everything runs on your phone. There is no server: your emails are read by the app on your device and sent only to Google's Gemini API, using your own API key, to recognise events.
